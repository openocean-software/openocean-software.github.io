## Overview

WHOI-ROS is a collection of shared ROS 2 software libraries and tools developed by the Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution for underwater vehicle operations.

It is the next-generation evolution of WHOI robotics software, migrated from the earlier DS_ROS (Deep Submergence ROS) framework. Source is on the [WHOI GitLab](https://git.whoi.edu/whoi-ros).

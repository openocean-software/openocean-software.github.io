    How member stacks connect today

    What we know about each bridge between member stacks today. Send corrections on the forum. Source: middleware interoperability goal in openocean-plan.

    Bridge Status Maintainer Notes
    Goby ↔ MOOS Mature GobySoft Built into Goby3. The one production-grade bridge.
    Goby ↔ ROS 2 Demo GobySoft goby_ros_gateway. Not yet used outside demo code.
    ROS 2 ↔ MOOS Ad hoc Various No single maintained bridge. Labs keep custom solutions.
    LCM ↔ ROS 2 Ad hoc Various lcm_to_ros packages pinned to specific versions.
    LCM ↔ MOOS Research-grade Various Published bridge (IEEE, 2019), not actively maintained.
